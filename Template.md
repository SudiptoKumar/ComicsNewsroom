# ComicsNewsroom V3 Post Templates

The renderer chooses a template dynamically. The channel should feel like one editorial brand without forcing every story into the same shape.

## Major Announcement

```text
✦ MAJOR ANNOUNCEMENT

[headline]

[one-sentence summary]

✦ [key fact]
✦ [key fact]
✦ [key fact]

💡 Why fans care: [optional]

Source: [source]
```

## Trailer / PV

```text
🎬 NEW TRAILER

[headline]

[one-sentence summary]

▶ Watch Trailer 👉 YouTube
```

When a second official platform is verified:

```text
▶ YouTube · Crunchyroll
```

## Manga → Anime

```text
◆ MANGA → ANIME

[headline]

[summary]

✦ Studio: ...
✦ Format: ...
✦ Release: ...

Source: ...
```

## Marvel / DC

```text
◆ MARVEL

[headline]

[summary]

✦ [key fact]

```


## FAN EXTRA

FAN EXTRA is intentionally occasional, source-grounded and never used to fill a weak news cycle.

Supported types include:

```text
Quick Fact
Hidden Detail
Franchise Timeline
Creator Spotlight
Fan Guide
Origin Story
Why It Matters
Did You Know
```
